
Parag Agrawal is a highly accomplished technology executive with deep experience in software engineering, AI, and data systems.
He joined Twitter in 2011 as a Distinguished Software Engineer, became Chief Technology Officer (CTO) in 2017, and was appointed CEO in 2021, succeeding Jack Dorsey.
During his tenure, he led the company's engineering strategy, machine learning initiatives, and platform infrastructure.
Following Twitter's acquisition by Elon Musk in 2022, Agrawal transitioned into entrepreneurship and founded AI startup Parallel Web Systems, focused on building infrastructure for AI-powered web search and intelligent agents.
The company has since attracted significant investment and strategic partnerships.
